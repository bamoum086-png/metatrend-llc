Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Meta trend LLC ("we", "us"), a New Mexico limited liability company, collects and uses information when you visit this website or work with us.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you send us directly, such as your name, email address, phone number, company name and project details when you contact us by email or WhatsApp.
  • Files and materials you share for a project, such as product footage, images, scripts and brand assets.
  • Billing information needed to issue invoices and receive payments. Payments are processed by our payment providers; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Basic technical data collected by our hosting provider and by embedded video players (Vimeo, Google Drive), such as IP address, browser type and pages viewed.

2. How we use information

  • To reply to your requests and provide our video and social media services.
  • To prepare, deliver and revise the work you order.
  • To send invoices, receive payments and keep business and tax records.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run the business (for example email, file storage, video hosting, payment and banking providers) and only as needed for those services, or when the law requires it.

4. Client materials

Footage and assets you send us are used only for your project. We may show finished work in our portfolio unless you ask us not to.

5. Retention

We keep project files and messages for as long as needed to provide the services and support revisions, and business records for as long as the law requires.

6. Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by contacting us. We will reply within a reasonable time.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
